## 💡 Why This Project?

Building and replicating the MicroDuck robot often comes with hidden hurdles—ranging from communication frame drops under heavy loads and confusing PCB stackup traps, to unexpected SMT assembly fees and wiring confusion.

This repository documents **hands-on, real-world engineering experience** to help you bypass every pitfall:
- 🛠️ **Troubleshooting**: Fixing original hardware bugs and improving communication stability.
- 💰 **Cost Optimization**: Step-by-step guides for cost-effective PCB prototyping and smart BOM management.
- 🔌 **Beginner Friendly**: Enhanced silkscreens, clear wiring diagrams, and assembly checklists.
